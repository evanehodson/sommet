PHASE 02 — RACE DAY TELEMETRY & SAFETY

Backcountry Tracking & Zero-Latency Ingestion

We deploy self-healing radio mesh gateways and lightweight RFID mats across cell-dead zones — giving you frequent intermediate splits, live spectator tracking, and automated safety alerts.

Deployable LoRa Mesh Network
Battery-powered radio relays dropped every 2–3 miles in backcountry gaps to eliminate 10-mile dark zones without relying on cell towers or HAM operators.
High-Density Intermediate Checkpoints
Low-cost, lightweight RFID sensors and radio nodes placed between major aid stations so split updates drop every few miles instead of every few hours.
Automated Safety & Stalled-Runner Alerts
Automatic system pings if an athlete's split is overdue between nodes, giving Race Directors and SAR teams immediate last-known-location data.
Offline Local Storage Buffer
Dual-frequency RFID mats store all chip passes locally and auto-sync to the cloud the moment a gateway connects, guaranteeing 100% data retention.

High-Density Split Coverage

HAM radio and standard timing chips only report from major aid stations. We deploy lightweight, weather-proof RFID readers and LoRa mesh relays in deep backcountry sections to give you frequent split updates and continuous safety tracking.

  • Deployable mini-RFID mats between aid stations
  • LoRa mesh relays over zero-connectivity terrain
  • Automatic stalled-runner detection

Backcountry Live Tracking

Redundant tracking where cell service fails. LoRa mesh nodes at deep split points combine with satellite and cellular into a single live feed with real-time telemetry.

  • Custom long-range LoRa mesh network deployment
  • Hybrid ingest: cellular + satellite + RF
  • Safety dashboard with stalled-runner alerts

Instant Leaderboards & Results
